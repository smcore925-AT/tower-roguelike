# Design

Living design notes. The owner decides; agents may add proposals marked **(proposal)**.

## Pitch

A fast-paced 3D action roguelike about climbing a tower. Every floor is different and harder than the last.
Explore each floor, fight intense battles, collect loot, and push as high as you can before you die.

## Pillars

1. **Intense, responsive combat.** Moment-to-moment feel matters most.
2. **Every floor feels different.** New layouts, themes, enemies, or rules as you climb.
3. **Loot that changes how you play.** Items combine into builds over a run.
4. **Rising tension.** Difficulty climbs steadily floor by floor.

## Settled so far

- 3D world, possibly with 2D (sprite) art: see `DECISIONS.md`.
- Roguelike structure: runs end on death.
- Floors get progressively harder.

## Open questions

- What is the tower's unique twist? (rules that change per floor, a shrinking tower, path choices, something else)
- What carries over between runs, if anything?
- Camera: fixed angle (isometric or 3/4) or free camera?
- Art: 2D sprites in 3D, low-poly 3D, or pixel art?
- Player: one character or several? Melee, ranged, or both?
- How many floors in a full run? Bosses every N floors?

## First playable slice (target)

One character, about 3 enemy types, 1 boss, 5 floors, about 20 items.
Get this feeling good before adding more content.
